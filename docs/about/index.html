About

Hi, I'm Trish Leto.

GenX UGC creator and model, U.S. Navy veteran and public speaker, based in Tampa, Florida.

I'm a VERY proud U.S. Navy veteran. I was a helicopter mechanic, so trust me, I can follow a process and I show up when it counts.

My why? Storytelling. I believe it's the most powerful thing we can do, because it lets us use our trials and tribulations as building blocks for prosperity.

Part of my story is sobriety. The hardest part wasn't giving up alcohol. It was learning to forgive myself. Once I did, I stepped back into my purpose and started making content that mattered, sharing my story from my HEART so other people know it's OK to not be OK. Every day I make the choice not to drink, and instead build a business around my story.

Now I make short-form videos for brands about products I ACTUALLY use. Supplements, kitchen gadgets, skin care, my morning coffee: if I use it, I can show it on camera exactly the way it fits my life. I'm GenX, and maybe your customers are too. That's my lane: real talk for real grown-ups.

And honestly? I'm addicted to seeing other people be successful. That's why I teach, on stages and in my guides. GenX or Boomer, you CAN do this!
