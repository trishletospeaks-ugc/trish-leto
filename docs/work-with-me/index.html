Ground rules

What I don't do

A few hard NOs. Saves us both an email!

  • Alcohol, tobacco or gamblingNot for any budget.
  • LingerieI could. I choose not to.
  • Managing your brand accountsI don't run or post to your brand accounts, and I don't start new accounts for you (“canvas UGC”). I make the content. You post it.
  • Fake reviewsI only talk about products I have actually used.
  • Kids' facesKids on camera are hands only, never faces.
  • Unpaid collabsPAID collabs ONLY. Gifted product alone is not a deal.

Questions

Good questions. STRAIGHT answers.

What does it cost?

Every job is a custom quote. It depends on how many videos you need, how much b-roll, and where and for how long you will run them. Email me the details and I will send a quote.

Do you post the videos for us?

Not on your brand accounts, and I don't start new accounts for you. You get the files and post or run them wherever you like.

Can we run your videos as ads?

Yes. Tell me where and for how long when you ask for a quote, and it goes into the quote. Not sure what to ask for? Read UGC usage rights and how TikTok Spark Ads work. If you want the ads run for you too, ask about a UGC + ads package.

Which categories are a fit?

Supplements, food and drink, pets, cars, kitchen and appliances, plants and gardening, makeup, skin care and hair care. Not a fit: alcohol, tobacco, gambling and lingerie.

How do the videos get to us?

Through a shared Google Drive folder, batch by batch.
